# Variant Ideas

## Solus Mine

* Invalidating Mine
    * Disables your inventory
* Nullifying Mine
    * Afflicts 1 permanent nullify buildup
* Nuke
    * Massive mine, moves slowly, kills anything within a 4m radius upon detonation
    * Note from TS: solus mine is a bit badly designed in that if a melee tries to counter play by breaking it they get punished, unfortunately. Be wary of making them too punishing cause they're prone for "not my fault" deaths.

* Slammer

    * Idea from Abbas

## Solus Extractor

* Cloaked Extractor
    * Cloaked while out of combat
    * Idea from Eric

* Cleptomaniac Extractor
    * Disables duplicating skill
    * as a result, it keeps stealing items
    * Won't run away due to disabling duplication skill
    * Idea from Abbas

## Solus Distributor

* Proto-Distributor
    * Spawns exclusively variants of whatever it spawns

* Probe Distributor
    * Spawns solus probes

## Solus Scorcher

* Napalm Scorcher
    * Oil pool leaves a DOT field

* Oilspill Scorcher
    * Only fires oil, lower cooldown

## Solus Transporter

* Voided Transporter
    * Fires a Void Jailer death projectile
